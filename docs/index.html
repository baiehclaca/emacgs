Laplace Transformer

Pattern-matching AI, running in your browser

1. Pattern match

Score every position in the haystack by how strongly the needle attends to it through the Laplace kernel exp(-‖q−k‖₁ / b).

2. Induction-head continuation

Classic induction-head algorithm: look at the tail of the input, find its strongest 2-gram match earlier in the context via the Laplace kernel, and copy what came next. Works great on repeating patterns — try abcabcabc.

3. Attention heatmap

Full Tn × Th Laplace-attention map: rows are needle positions, columns are haystack positions. Brighter = stronger match.
